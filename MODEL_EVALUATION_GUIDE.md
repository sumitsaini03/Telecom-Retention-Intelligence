# Model evaluation guide

## What population was evaluated?

The code makes one stratified 80/20 split with seed 42. The training partition has 5,634 records and the untouched holdout has 1,409 records, including 374 observed churn labels (26.54%). Both models use the same split. Five-fold cross-validation chooses model parameters using training data only. Preprocessing is inside the pipeline and is refit within each training fold.

The classification thresholds were selected by maximizing F1 on five-fold out-of-fold training predictions: 0.32 for logistic regression and 0.34 for XGBoost. The holdout was not used to select thresholds. These thresholds do not encode contact capacity, intervention cost, or the cost of a missed churner.

## Metric meanings

| Metric | Plain-language meaning | XGBoost holdout result |
|---|---|---:|
| ROC-AUC | Chance that a randomly selected churn case ranks above a randomly selected retained case across thresholds. 0.5 is chance ranking; 1 is perfect ranking. | 0.8465 |
| Average precision | Summary of precision across recall levels for the churn class; compare it with the positive prevalence. It is not necessarily the trapezoidal area under a plotted PR curve. | 0.6603 (prevalence 0.2654) |
| Precision | Of customers classified as churn, the share with an observed churn label. | 0.5645 |
| Recall | Of customers with an observed churn label, the share classified as churn. | 0.7139 |
| F1 | Harmonic mean of precision and recall at the chosen threshold. | 0.6305 |
| Accuracy | Share of all holdout rows classified correctly; interpret beside class balance. | 0.7779 |
| Specificity | Share of retained customers correctly classified as retained. | 0.8010 |
| Brier score | Mean squared probability error; smaller is better, but this single score does not establish calibration quality. | 0.1351 |

The confusion matrix at threshold 0.34 is TN=829, FP=206, FN=107, TP=267. The bootstrap 95% interval for XGBoost ROC-AUC is 0.8251–0.8681. Logistic regression has ROC-AUC 0.8417 and average precision 0.6295 on the same holdout. The ROC-AUC intervals overlap, and the project did not run a paired significance test, so the small ROC-AUC difference does not establish a decisive winner.

## Calibration and interpretation

`outputs/holdout_calibration.png` compares mean predicted probability to observed churn frequency in quantile bins. `outputs/metrics.json` also reports Brier scores. Neither plot nor score guarantees calibration on future customers; validation uses one random sample from a single historical snapshot.

`outputs/roc_pr_curves.png`, `outputs/model_comparison.png`, `outputs/holdout_confusion_matrix.png`, `outputs/holdout_probability_by_outcome.png`, and `outputs/training_oof_threshold_tradeoff.png` are generated by `python src/analysis.py`. The threshold trade-off chart uses training OOF scores; the holdout figures are evaluation only.

## Limitations

- The sample has no timestamps for a later-period validation split, and the model is not validated for future production use.
- The threshold maximizes F1 rather than expected business value.
- A churn prediction is not evidence that an offer will prevent churn. The sample contains no randomized offer or retention outcomes.
- The model is retrospective and includes fields such as tenure and accumulated charges from the same snapshot as the target; its results should not be presented as prospective campaign lift.
- The holdout is reused for the displayed model interpretation and descriptive score diagnostics. It remains separate from hyperparameter and threshold selection.
